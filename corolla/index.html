ABS X-ray exposes the animated valve plungers and coils by hiding their enclosing passages and sleeves. Click and drag to orbit. Scroll to zoom. ABS ON selects the ABS-modulation animation. Apply brakes holds the brakes on and continuously repeats the active ABS or normal-braking animation. Release brakes stops and resets. Pause freezes the current frame.

Teaching visualization, not Toyota factory CAD or validated diagnostic specifications. Brake and ABS movements are exaggerated for visibility. Internet access is required for the 3D library.
