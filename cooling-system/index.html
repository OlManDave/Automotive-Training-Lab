Automatic regulation is the normal driving lesson: temperature rises from cold, the thermostat starts opening near 82°C (80–84°C nominal) and is fully open by 95°C, while a healthy engine remains in the 82–104°C operating range. Select a fault to see how warm-up and overheating change. Manual temperature demo is an override for inspecting thermostat movement; use Automatic regulation to test faults.
