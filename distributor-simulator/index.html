One distributor revolution

One distributor revolution equals two crankshaft revolutions. The main diagram turns at a steady rate. The electrical timing shown is illustrative. On a real distributor, the spark follows points opening as soon as voltage can jump the gaps.

Teaching schematic. The example coil shows the RUN circuit with the windings sharing coil +. The voltage display models a 12 V supply, a 1.5 Ω ballast and a 1.5 Ω coil primary. It shows the RUN feed voltage trend and omits fast switching transients. Contact gaps and timing are illustrative.
