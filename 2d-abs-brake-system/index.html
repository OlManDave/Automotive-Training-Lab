Simplified conventional recirculating ABS. Two diagonal hydraulic circuits remain isolated; a shared motor drives one return-pump element per circuit. Low-pressure return chambers are inside the HCU. The external high-pressure accumulator and mechanical proportioning valve have been removed. EBD and ESP functions are not simulated. ABS operation reference.

ABS demonstration

Inlet / outlet valve states

The master cylinder follows the pedal and stays applied during ABS. Each selected wheel cycles independently. In normal braking, inlet valves are open and outlet valves are closed; the return pump is off.

ABS is commanded manually for learning. Timing and pressures are illustrative; wheel-speed and tyre-slip dynamics are not simulated. Pressure colour can remain visible when net flow has stopped.
