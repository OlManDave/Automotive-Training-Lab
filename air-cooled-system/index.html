Cutaway teaching diagram of cooling shrouds, internal distribution vanes, flap guides, and lower cylinder deflector tins. Illustrated fan air split is approximately 2/3 to heads and 1/3 to cylinders, not a measured specification for every VW engine. Missing lower tins cause air to bypass lower cooling fins and create local hot spots. The light-tinted cover panels are shown transparent so the flow paths remain visible. Unlike a water-cooled vehicle, this design has no radiator, coolant, thermostat, or electric temperature-controlled fan. Oil-temperature bands shown here are teaching ranges for this air-cooled example. Cylinder-head metal temperatures are substantially higher and vary with sensor location and engine design. Thermal dynamics are illustrative, not manufacturer calibration.
